## Interact with a canvas

### Move an item

*   Click and drag the item around the canvas
*   Press space bar on its Move button to pick the item up, arrow keys to move, and space bar to drop

### Resize an item

*   Click and drag the bottom right corner of the item
*   Press space bar on its Resize button, arrow keys to resize, and space bar to confirm

## Move between lists

*   Click and drag item to new list
*   Press item's Move button to open a menu of available lists, arrow keys to select a list, Enter to drop, or Escape to cancel

## Sort a list

*   Use Tab/Shift+Tab to navigate to an item
*   Press Space to enter into drag mode
*   Use Down/Right, Up/Left arrow keys to select a new position
*   Press Space to drop item in new position

## Sort a listbox

*   Use arrow keys to select an option
*   Press Space to enter into drag mode
*   Use arrow keys to select a new position
*   Press Space to drop option in new position

See also the [Lightning Design System's Accessibility Patterns](https://lightningdesignsystem.com/accessibility/patterns/listbox) documentation for listboxes.
