_Blank screens. No messages, no data, no content. At first glance, empty states seem like insignificant, temporary moments. But in reality, they hold great potential. They’re often the user’s first impression of a feature — or even the entire product — and how you handle that empty state can determine whether they stick around or move on. How can you turn an empty state into a positive experience that encourages action and engagement?_

This article explores how to approach empty states as opportunities rather than placeholders. By providing guidance, setting clear expectations, and offering meaningful next steps, designers can turn blank screens into valuable moments that drive user interaction.

## Why Empty States Matter

Empty states aren’t just an absence of content; they’re a chance to establish clarity, build trust, and guide users forward.

### The first impression counts

For many features — like a new inbox, a fresh dashboard, or a just-launched profile page — the initial empty state is the first thing users see. A blank screen with no guidance can feel unfinished or unwelcoming. A thoughtfully designed empty state, on the other hand, can make the product feel intentional and well-considered from the start.

### Clarifying the next step

Users encountering an empty state often wonder what they’re supposed to do next. Clear, concise messaging and visual cues help them understand the purpose of the feature and how to get started.

### Building user confidence

An empathetic, well-structured empty state reassures users that they’re on the right track. By acknowledging that it’s okay to start with nothing and showing them how to move forward, you help them feel more confident in using the product.
