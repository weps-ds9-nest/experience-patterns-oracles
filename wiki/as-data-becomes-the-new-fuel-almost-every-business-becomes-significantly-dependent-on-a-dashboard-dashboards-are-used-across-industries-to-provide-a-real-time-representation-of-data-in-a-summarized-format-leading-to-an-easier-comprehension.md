## Categorize your personas into roles

Your target persona is based on market research and demographics which are 
usually created while identifying the target audience. A dashboard design a
allows you to summarize data in the most optimized format which allows you 
to cater to the audience effectively.

Unlike other applications, a dashboard does not cater to each persona, but 
to each role.

Filter your target audience into personas and create a comprehensive list o
of each persona. Then, categorize each persona into roles and create a dash
dashboard for each role and NOT for each persona. This will enable you to c
create an accurate user experience for your target audience.

## Acknowledge the limitations

Dashboard designs are to be created after understanding limitations, both, 
business and technological. Create the most feasible user experience after 
acknowledging the current atmosphere of challenges. Understand your product

to. It becomes imperative to create a design that effectively portrays the
