The fastest way to lose a user? Silence. And silence is exactly what most empty states sound like.

Think: “No messages yet,” “No saved items,” “No notifications.” Silence.

But here’s the thing: they don’t have to be blank. When done right, they can reassure, motivate, and guide the next step.

This is how I approached rewriting empty states for the same fictional e-commerce app, **_Anoriv._**

## The Challenge

Most empty states read like placeholders:

• Cart empty: “No items.”

• Wishlist empty: “You don’t have any favorites.”

• Notifications empty: “No notifications.”

They’re technically correct, but emotionally flat. There is no guidance, no encouragement, no sense of brand voice. Users have to figure out what to do next, or worse, leave the app.

## My Approach

I applied the same UX writing principles I used for the push notifications, but tuned them for moments of absence.

### General Principles I Applied

• Acknowledge the moment → Shows we understand where the user is.

• Guide the next step → Doesn’t leave them stuck; points to action.

• Add warmth → A touch of personality turns emptiness into invitation.

• Keep it concise → One line of reassurance, one line of direction.

## Rewrites: Before & After

### 1. Empty Cart

Before: “No items.”

After: “Your cart is waiting to be filled 🛒

Tap ‘Shop Now’ to start your haul.”

Here’s why: **Empathy + direction**. Emoji softens the void, CTA guides the next step.

### 2. Empty Wishlist

Before: “You don’t have any favorites.”

After: “Nothing saved yet 💖

See something you love? Tap the heart to keep it here.”

Here’s why: **Teaching moment**. It turns a dead end into a tip, teaching as it reassures.

### 3. Empty Notifications

Before: “No notifications.”

After: “All quiet for now 🔔

We’ll let you know when deals drop or items are back.”

Here’s why: **Reassurance**. The user learns that they won’t miss out; the app is working for them in the background.

## Anoriv Empty State Style Guide

### 1. Voice & Tone

• **Voice**: Friendly and helpful, like a store assistant who notices you looking lost and points you in the right direction.

• **Tone**: Calm and encouraging, not pushy, not scolding.

### 2. Structure

Each empty state has:

• **Acknowledgment** → “Nothing saved yet” / “All quiet.”

• **Encouragement / Tip** → “Tap the heart to keep it here.”

• **CTA** (where relevant) → “Shop Now,” “Browse collections.”

### 3. When to Add Personality

• Use emojis or playful phrasing sparingly. They should feel like a warm smile, not a performance.

## Takeaway

I’m learning that empty states aren’t really empty. They’re either a wall or a doorway. Writing decides which, and I’m still practicing how to open more doors.
