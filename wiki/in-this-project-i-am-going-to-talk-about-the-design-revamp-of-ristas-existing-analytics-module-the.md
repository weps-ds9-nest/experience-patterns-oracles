## Why we did it?

RISTA POS was developed 7 years ago in which the UI followed a very basic yet very functional structure. But post acquisition, RISTA had a very good opportunity to leverage the merchant base of DotPe and expand their reach. But to Pitch RISTA to merchant, we needed to come up with a more refreshed version of it. Not just by redesigning the UI but also finding opportunities to improve the overall experience much better.

I encountered several challenges during this project, and one of the significant hurdles was the **tight timeline**. ### Interview Takeaways: Owners

1.   Many owners prefer **compact devices such as smartphones and tablets** to access the dashboard rather than laptops or large monitors, as they are typically occupied with meetings and constantly on the move.
2.   A subset of these owners has prior experience with various POS systems like **Petpooja, Limetray, and Posist**, and they find RISTA’s user interface to be quite simplistic.
3.   The frequency of dashboard usage varies depending on the type of business. On average, owners access it **two to three times a week.**
4.   The primary purpose of using the dashboard is to track the performance of their businesses across multiple locations, including **sales and revenue data, trend analysis, and comparisons with historical data.**
5.   Owners regularly share this data with their **business teams and other stakeholders** to facilitate better planning for the future.
6.   Some owners have raised concerns about the **app crashes and unresponsive behaviour**

### Interview Takeaways: Managers

1.   The majority of managers prefer using devices like **laptops or large monitor screens** to access the dashboard because they typically carry out these tasks during their work hours within the restaurant premises.
2.   Only a small number of managers have prior experience with POS systems like Petpooja, and they believe that RISTA’s interface could benefit from some enhancements.
3.   Managers use the dashboard **daily, and sometimes even more frequently on busy days or during festive occasions**, to closely monitor the overall performance of their respective stores.
4.   The primary focus of managers when using the dashboard is to assess the performance of the specific store they are responsible for. This involves **tracking sales and revenue, conducting trend analysis**, and comparing current data with historical records.
5.   Managers regularly share this performance data with their peers and the owners to facilitate **regular store performance reviews**. However, some managers occasionally **forget to send these reports due to the demanding schedule** within the restaurant.
6.   Additionally, some managers have experienced issues with the app becoming **unresponsive** on multiple occasions, necessitating them to reinstall the app in order to regain access to the data.
7.   some of them have used excel as well to generate analytical reports in the past

## Success Metrics

After onboarding over 200+ merchants, we saw a significant increase in the engagement time on the dashboard.

1.   Average spent time on the dashboard increased from **1min 15 secs to 4 min 30 seconds**
2.   Average user per minute increased from **4 to 11 users**.
