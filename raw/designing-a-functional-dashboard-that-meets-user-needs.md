> **Brief**:
> 
>  Design a dashboard that will provide administrators with secure login access, enabling them to monitor key metrics and gain real-time insights into the product’s performance.

EscrowCentral is an innovative Escrow Management System that revolutionizes the way businesses and individuals handle financial transactions. It simplifies the escrow process and empowers users to generate secure payment links effortlessly.

**Goal:**

1.   Track Progress of every activity
2.   See progress and churn rate
3.   Identify Insights and Trends
4.   Gain real-time visibility into essential metrics and data

> Design Process:
> 
>  I used the Design Thinking Process: Research, Define, Ideate, Prototype and Test.

With the Design Thinking Framework in mind, I began the research by dedicating substantial effort to studying the Escrow system comprehensively. This involved gaining a deep understanding of its underlying principles, user demographics, and the motivations behind users’ adoption of the system. 

 Also, I conducted extensive secondary desk research, thoroughly analyzing and filtering through relevant reports and findings to inform the design process effectively.

The research helped me identify key metrics that were deemed non-negotiable and crucial for the admin dashboard; these were:

1.   **Wallet Balance**: the rationale behind prominently positioning at the top of the dashboard was to provide administrators with immediate visibility into the volume of transactions being processed. By having this information readily available, admins could gauge their performance and assess how effectively they were managing funds.
2.   **Escrow Fee Revenue:** this enables admin assess the financial health of the platform as this represents the profit generated.

 This insight provides valuable information on the overall profitability of the business and helps administrators make informed decisions regarding pricing, fee structures, and revenue optimization strategies.
3.   **Completed Transactions:** this metric represents the successful transactions processed through Escrow Central. Administrators could easily track the volume of completed transactions over a specific period. This information provides valuable insights into the platform’s usage and adoption.
4.   **No. of Users:** this represents the total count of registered users on the Escrow Central platform. This serves as a valuable benchmark for measuring the platform’s success and evaluating the rate of its market penetration.
5.   **Active Region:** this data allows administrators to identify regions with the highest demand for escrow services, target specific geographical markets for growth and expansion, and tailor marketing and support strategies accordingly.
6.   **Escrow Dispute Cases:** this metric helps administrators monitor the frequency and severity of dispute cases, giving insights into potential pain points or issues in the platform’s workflow, user communication, or dispute resolution processes. Doing this enhances users trust and satisfaction.
7.   **Category:** The Category metric represents the different categories or types of transactions within the platform. This information helps administrators understand the platform’s usage patterns, identify popular transaction categories, and make data-driven decisions regarding resource allocation, marketing strategies, and customer support.

These metrics were carefully selected as they align seamlessly with the goals of the Escrow Central platform. By focusing on these metrics, I ensured that the admin dashboard delivers the most relevant and impactful data, enabling administrators to make informed decisions, track progress, and drive the growth and profitability of Escrow Central effectively.

**Wireframe:**Throughout the wireframing process, I prioritized usability and clarity over excessive visuals. I carefully placed each metric, used charts and graphs sparingly, and maintained a clear hierarchy of information. 

 The goal was to strike a balance between aesthetics and functionality, ensuring that administrators could quickly understand the insights without feeling overwhelmed. By iterating over and over again, I refined the wireframes to create a visually engaging and user-friendly admin dashboard for Escrow Central.

Using Figma, I developed a minimalist style guide for high-fidelity designs. To further enhance the experience, I animated some of the elements. The combination of minimalism and interactivity resulted in a visually appealing and user-friendly experience for administrators.
