# An illustration showing a woman pointing at a dashboard with graphs

A well-designed dashboard is essential for effective data visualization and
and decision-making. This article provides a comprehensive guide on how to 
design dashboards that meet user needs and goals.

## Key Patterns
* Define the people & their purpose
* Choose the right type of dashboard (monitoring, interactive analytic, or 
navigational)
* Display the right data in the right way (data granularity, time and compa
comparison)
* Organize logically and progressively
* Enhance with styling (colors and fonts)
