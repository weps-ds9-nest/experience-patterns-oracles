Are you tired of the vague and unhelpful “click here” button that seems to populate every website and app? As UX designers, it’s our responsibility to create interfaces that are intuitive, user-friendly, and provide a seamless experience. In this blog, we’ll discuss the problem of the “click here” button and explore solutions to make the user journey more enjoyable and effective.

**The Problem: Lack of Clarity**

The “click here” button lacks descriptive text, leaving users guessing about what action they are taking. Additionally, it can hinder accessibility for users with screen readers or assistive technology, as it provides little context. This results in a poor user experience and a decrease in conversions.

**The Solution: Descriptive and Actionable Language**

One solution is to use descriptive and actionable language in call to action buttons. Instead of “click here,” we can use phrases like “download the PDF,” “sign up for a free trial,” or “add to cart.” By providing clear and concise language, we can make the user’s journey more enjoyable and effective.

**The Solution: Design Elements**

Another solution is to use design elements to draw attention to the call to action. This can include the use of color or contrast, making the button more noticeable and easier to find. Additionally, using animation or hover effects can make the button more engaging and interactive.

**The Solution: A/B Testing**

A/B testing is an effective way to test different call to action buttons and determine which one works best for your audience. By creating multiple variations of the button and testing them with real users, you can gather data on which option is more effective and make data-driven decisions.

**The Solution: Be Creative**

Let’s take this opportunity to showcase our creativity and come up with alternative phrases for “click here” that are more engaging and descriptive. For example, “Get your free trial,” “Join the conversation,” or “Start your journey.” By using creative language, we can make the user journey more enjoyable and memorable.
