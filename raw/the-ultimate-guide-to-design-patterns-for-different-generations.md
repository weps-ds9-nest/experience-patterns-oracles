## Discover the importance of Design Patterns and how they impact different generations

Photo by Kenny Eliason on Unsplash

For decades, design patterns have persisted, and their progress has been dependent on shifting technological consumption habits across the age split.

The significant shift in how people interact with technology through time has forced designers to adapt their design patterns to suit each subsequent generation.

Understanding design patterns that appeal to each generation may help you create more engaging and relevant designs that successfully engage your target audience. The following discussion will elaborate on the ultimate guidebook for design patterns throughout generations.

## Baby Boomers

Photo by Fona

Baby boomers are not averse to technology despite common belief. In fact, they are enthusiastic about its potential to maintain their independence, establish a lasting legacy, and remain connected with family and friends.

They are willing to make substantial investments in products that enhance their quality of life and provide peace of mind during a time of potential difficulty.

One technology that has resonated particularly well with this demographic is smart home technology, as it aligns with their values of autonomy and connectivity.

As the body ages, its physical capabilities naturally decline, particularly in areas such as vision, motor skills, and memory. To support boomers, designers of technology products should make a concerted effort to address these limitations.

Failure to do so can result in feelings of frustration and anger among this group. It is thus a social responsibility of businesses that cater to this demographic to choose their design elements thoughtfully.

A few design patterns to address these issues should be considered. For instance, text size should be above average for digital products, with attention paid to contrast and color.
