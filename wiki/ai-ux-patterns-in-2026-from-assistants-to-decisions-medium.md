> **Summary:**AI UX patterns are no longer novel — they’re infrastructure. What matters now isn’t _which_ patterns you use, but _how much responsibility_ they push back onto designers as AI shifts from helper to decision-maker.

Products look cleaner. AI feels faster. Demos are smoother.

Yet teams feel less confident.

Not because AI is failing — but because many patterns now sit directly on top of decision-making. When something breaks, it’s unclear _where judgment failed_. AI UX patterns aren’t an inspiration anymore. They’re a responsibility map.
