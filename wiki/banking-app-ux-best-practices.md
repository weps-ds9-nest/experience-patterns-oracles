# Banking App UX: Top 10 Best Practices

Trust critical in banking apps since users grant product managing money significant trust. Ten best practices ensure users feel safe: make things easy to find through solid information architecture and user research, lead by innovating rather than copying competitors, provide educational content for financial wellbeing, ensure clear communication around security regulations, create tailored experiences through personalization, ensure accessible and inclusive experiences, implement spending analytics helping users categorize spending, help clients reach goals through savings mechanisms and gamification, make fund transferring effortless especially between app users, provide lightning-fast support for time-sensitive issues. Security measures include encryption, 2FA, fraud detection/prevention, biometric authentication. AI/ML enable personalized experiences, fraud detection, chatbots/virtual assistants.

## Key Patterns & Concepts

- **Make Information Easy to Find**: Solid IA critical; users must easily access account balance, recent spending, analytics; conduct UX research; test design vigorously for frictionless experience
- **Lead by Innovation**: Avoid copying competitors; develop continuous innovation framework; understand intergenerational perception shifts around money
- **Provide Education**: Offer accessible, bite-sized financial education content; helps users make better decisions; valuable tool improving experience and loyalty
- **Clear Regulation Communication**: Explain security measures like 2FA; help users understanding importance; balance convenience with security; educate users on scams
- **Tailored Experiences**: Strategic, holistic personalization emphasizes value users receiving; build long-lasting relationships; mature personalization companies outperform low-maturity by 189%
- **Accessibility & Inclusion**: Use simple accessible language, inclusive imagery, gender-neutral copy, remove clutter, ask only essential information, include progress bars, provide auto-completion
- **Spending Analytics**: Categorize spending helping users make informed decisions; don't force manual transaction review and math
- **Help Reach Goals**: Support saving via jars rounding daily balance, saving plans, comparing spending to average months, prompts cutting spending, gamification (points, levels, leaderboards, badges, progress)
- **Effortless Fund Transfer**: Make sharing bills and splitting transactions swift; if both users same app, identify person vs card info copying
- **Lightning-Fast Support**: Ensure payment issues resolvable immediately via phone/live chat; time-sensitive payments critical
- **Data Encryption**: Use advanced encryption techniques (HTTPS) protecting sensitive information from unauthorized access
- **Two-Factor Authentication**: Adds security layer requiring identity verification through second factor (code, biometric); balance with UX via streamlined processes
- **Fraud Detection & Prevention**: Leverage AI/ML identifying and preventing fraudulent activities proactively; real-time monitoring
- **Biometric Authentication**: Enable seamless authentication via fingerprints/facial recognition; quick, secure, eliminates passwords/PINs
- **Personalized Experiences**: AI-powered algorithms analyze behavior/preferences delivering tailored recommendations
- **Chatbots/Virtual Assistants**: Provide 24/7 support via AI answering queries, resolving issues, automating routine tasks
- **Cross-Platform Consistency**: Maintain consistent experience across web, mobile, tablet platforms

## Full Article

Designing banking apps demanding given money's complex, important nature. Person choosing digital product managing money grants significant trust—even smallest design/experience hiccups undermine trust.

### State of Banking UX

Banking apps late to mobile boom. Earliest (2009) had few features geared balance inquiries, ATM/branch locations. Ten years later, apps standard. Per ABA report, 87% institutions with $50M-$15B assets developed/released apps. Incentive clear—companies investing considerable effort/resources sign of overwhelming acceptance/trust.

Skyrocketing adoption nowhere near halting. Over 3 billion people using digital banking—number projected continuing growing.

### UX Principles Banking Apps Should Consider

Creating usable, enjoyable UI revolves around intuitive user experience. Primary goal: design product not placing too much cognitive load, ensuring learning curve not too steep.

Worth paying attention to: making app evoke positive emotions. We all care for joyful experiences.

**Make Things Easy to Find**: Banking apps different shapes/forms but unified goal—managing people's finances. Important info like balance, spending, analytics should be easily accessible.

Critical: solid information architecture (IA). Designers must know how users think about categories and navigate niche.

To ensure IA rooted in correct user research interpretation, test vigorously. Usability, discoverability, findability, "-ility" issues surface, guiding toward better, frictionless experience.

**Lead by Innovating**: Banks weren't fast adopting digital experiences; can be conservative innovating. Many bank apps just copy competitors' products; more daring brands shine with new features.

Being competitive involves framework geared toward continuous innovation. Also relates to ever-changing intergenerational money perception—we agree what money is, but people different ages think differently about it.

**Provide Educational Content**: Financial education importance hasn't been grasped worldwide; not many educational systems focusing on providing it. Banks should aim providing accessible, bite-sized educational content helping people make better, sounder money decisions.

Valuable tool for product experience. Imagine service helping manage finances that also cares about financial wellbeing—valuable customer loyalty and satisfaction source.

**Ensure Clear Communication Around Regulations**: Financial services come lots regulation since dealing with vital resources. Regulations might limit product capability and experience ease. Although important for security, things like 2FA can create UX hiccups.

Important communicating security regulations importance. Sometimes unfair disadvantage, but by providing clear communication, can help users understanding added friction importance, building additional trust.

Valuable opportunity educating users on scams helping recognize/avoid them. Product should balance convenience and security.

**Create Tailored Experiences**: Banking products leveraging strategic, holistic personalization enjoying impressive ROI returns. By personalizing beyond basic information, apps emphasize user value received and build long-lasting relationships.

Worth mentioning: while deeply-personalized experience should be goal, safe saying companies should reach it in small steps. Demanding, time-consuming process; getting right through small victories way to go.

Per Blend study, ~75% financial sector decision-makers looking growing personalization investment; nearly 1/3 looking considerably increasing. Mature companies invested in data-driven personalization outperform those not doing so.

**Ensure Accessible and Inclusive Experience**: With accessibility becoming more prominent, dashboards must be inclusive for all users regardless ability.

Tips: Use simple, accessible language, inclusive images/illustrations/branding, gender-neutral copy, remove redundant pages/buttons/elements, ask only essential information keeping forms short, include progress bars, provide intuitive auto-completion.

**Spending Analytics**: Many people wonder how managing spending this much this week/month. Vital having system helping categorize spending allowing informed financial decisions.

No one should forced going through transactions manually doing math.

**Help Clients Reach Goals**: Banking apps help people making sense of money available. Beyond just buying-thing resource, money something people want having more of. People often setting goals saving for cars, apartments, computer gear.

Should have system helping people save. Common mechanism: saving jars rounding balance daily transferring surplus to piggy bank. Could have hints and saving plans.

Or have system helping users cut spending comparing financial activity to average month. Result: create valuable in-app prompts helping cut entertainment spending 10% allowing car purchase 3 months faster.

Wide array gamification features experimenting with—points, levels, leaderboards, badges, progress charts, many other features helping make product more engaging.

**Transferring Funds Shouldn't Be Bother**: One way or another, users inevitably situations sharing bills. Be it minor dinner or month-long road trip. Must make sure feature available in swift, effortless manner.

Currently, many users using third-party apps like Splitwise, then going trouble copying credit card info sending funds.

Potential making easier. If both users using same app, identify person rather than credit card, spare tedious personal/financial data copying.

**Provide Lightning-Fast Support**: One world's most frustrating things: banking app not working. Payments often very time-sensitive events; preventing transactions problem.

Situations happen; must make sure immediately resolvable issues through phone calls or live chat.
