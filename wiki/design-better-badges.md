# Design Better Badges

Badge design best practices focus on clarity and usability. Avoid long descriptions limiting to 1-2 words per badge. Distinguish badges from buttons when styling similarly to avoid confusion. Consider limiting notification badge numbers to two digits using "99+" for counts exceeding 99. Prioritize contrast over color relying on adequate contrast and readability. Don't use badges as headers or key indicators—maintain secondary, supportive role. Don't combine different-sized badges next to each other; use consistent sizing with color and iconography for differentiation.

## Key Patterns & Concepts

- **Content Limitation**: One or two words only; keep concise
- **Distinguish from Buttons**: Avoid similar styling; maintain clear visual differentiation
- **Notification Numbers**: Two-digit maximum; truncate to "99+" for larger counts
- **Contrast Priority**: Sufficient contrast between text and background; don't rely on color alone
- **Hierarchy**: Secondary and supportive to content; never primary indicators
- **Size Consistency**: Same size when displayed together; differentiate via color and iconography
- **Readability**: Easy to read at small sizes; clear typography
- **Visual Purpose**: Status indication, event markers, categorization, content tagging, notifications
