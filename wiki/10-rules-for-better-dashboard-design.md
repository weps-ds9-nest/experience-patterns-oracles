## Key Patterns
• Define the purpose of the dashboard
• Choose the right representation for the data
• Follow clear and consistent naming conventions
• Define the layout and flow
• Use building blocks with consistent structure
• Double your margins (white space)
• Don't hide information or rely on interactions too much
• Personalization rather than customization
• Interactive data tables or lists
• Design the dashboard last
