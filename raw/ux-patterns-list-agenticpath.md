## GenAI UX Design Patterns

AI introduces a new way for humans to interact with computers by focusing on intent-based outcome specification. Designing AI isn’t just about the model but it’s about the experience.

Gen AI design patterns help teams create products that are intuitive, trustworthy, and effective. They provide a shared language for product managers, data scientists, and interaction designers to collaborate seamlessly. By applying these patterns, we can bridge the gap between technical capabilities and user needs, making AI more useful, ethical, and human-centred.
