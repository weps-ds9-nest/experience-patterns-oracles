# 10 Grid System Mistakes Killing Your Dashboard Usability

When designing a dashboard grid system, it's essential to avoid common pitf
pitfalls that can negatively impact usability. This article highlights the 
top 10 mistakes to watch out for.

## Key Patterns

* Ignoring responsive design
* Overusing grids
* Failing to consider content hierarchy
* Not accounting for varying screen sizes
* Insufficient padding and margins
* Incorrect grid column usage
* Not providing adequate whitespace
* Failing to adjust font sizes and line heights
* Neglecting accessibility guidelines
* Inconsistent styling across different components
