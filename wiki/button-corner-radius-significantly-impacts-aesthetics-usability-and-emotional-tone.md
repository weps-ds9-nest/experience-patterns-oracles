# Button corner radius significantly impacts aesthetics, usability, and emotional tone.

Button corner radius significantly impacts aesthetics, usability, and emotional tone.

Button radius refers to the roundness of a button’s corners.

Below is an overview of different button radius options and their use cases, with real-world examples.

### 1. Sharp Radius (0px)

**Appearance:**

This button type has no corner rounding at all. The corners are perfectly square. These buttons give a formal and structured look to the interface.

**Use Cases**:

*   Corporate and professional designs (e.g., enterprise software, finance apps).
*   Minimalist/modern interfaces (clean, geometric layouts).

**Examples**

*   Jira
*   Microsoft Outlook
*   GitHub
*   Slack

### 2. Slightly Rounded Radius (2px–4px)

**Appearance:**

These buttons have a Subtle radius (2–4px). The seamless rounding is barely noticeable at first glance. They give a Professional look yet slightly softer than sharp corners.

**Use Cases:**

*   Fintech apps.
*   Productivity tools.
*   Designs that aim for clarity with a mature tone.
*   SaaS products
*   Dashboards and analytics interfaces

**Examples**

*   Notion
*   Slack
*   LinkedIn
