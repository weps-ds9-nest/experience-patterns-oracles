## Build future-proof products using the emerging AI UX standards.

AI is no longer “just a feature” or a “sparkle icon”. Integrating AI into your product changes what products are, how people expect them to behave, and what responsibilities designers & product teams carry.

### We’re seeing:

1.   **Users expecting adaptive**, multi-modal, _intelligent_ behavior.
2.   **Concerns about trust**, transparency, hallucinations, bias, misuse.
3.   **Usability issues**: vague prompts, ambiguous output, friction in refinement.

AI raises not only new possibilities, _but new pitfalls_. So product teams need patterns, repeatable, battle-tested solutions, to navigate the trade-offs.

[aiverse’s patterns](https://www.aiverse.design/patterns) is a useful toolkit in this space, based on **200+ real-world examples**, organized across five phases of the product journey.

## The 5 phases framework

Before we dive into the patterns, lets look at the overarching framework.

5 phase framework by aiverse.design — source author

Each AI interaction can be divided into 5 phases.

1.   **Onboarding —** how do you introduce AI, what can it do, with what limits.
2.   **Input —** how the user provide context: prompts, images, gestures, etc.
3.   **Output** — how results are delivered: formats (text, image, video), structure, preview, etc.
4.   **Refinement** — how users iteratively improve results: edit, regenerate, review, etc.
5.   **Learning** — how the system adapts over time: memory, personalization, feedback.

Each AI pattern that we’ve defined lies in one of these 5 phases. It’s essentially the best practices from other AI-native products made available for you to learn and implement in your own product.
