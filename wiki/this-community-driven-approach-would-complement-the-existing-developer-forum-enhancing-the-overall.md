### 2. Lack of Specificity and Visibility

Users often feel frustrated when they struggle to effectively utilize AI-powered tools, perceiving themselves as using a “smart” tool in a “dumb” way. This frustration stems from the pressure to keep up with AI hype and unmet personal expectations.

However, there is a key to unlocking AI’s potential: understanding that there is a desirable way to craft prompts. Specifically, effective AI utilization relies on:

> Asking the right questions and asking questions right.

The “right questions” aspect refers to identifying suitable use cases, as discussed earlier (e.g., expectation, relevant examples). “Asking questions right,” on the other hand, involves mastering the art of crafting optimal queries.

AI tools, in fact, operate based on success equations, which dictate the optimal approaches to achieving accurate results. By recognizing these principles and honing your prompt-crafting skills, users can harness AI’s power more efficiently. Ultimately, this requires **visibility into how AI processes queries**, empowering users to refine their approach and unlock AI’s full potential.

**(1) I have improved you AI, so now you better improve me**

Now it’s time for you to help me

As AI evolves with user feedback, it’s time for AI to return the favor. To create a harmonious partnership, we must address:

**The Key to Better Queries: Specificity**

AI needs user guidance, and users need help crafting effective queries. The answer lies in specificity. However, users often assume AI can read minds or possess common sense. As the saying goes: “Common sense is not common,” especially in AI interactions.

**Follow ups and refinement**

To overcome this challenge, AI should provide opportunities for refinement, requesting specific details to ensure users input queries with enough context. Currently, AI follow-ups often lack specificity, making it hard for users to learn and improve.

Meta AI asking for more details

Observing Meta AI’s interactions, I noticed that it rarely explicitly requests specific details. While it does ask follow-up questions, they often lack specificity, making it difficult to discern what went wrong or how to enhance previous queries.

To elevate this experience, consider taking it a step further: enhancing follow-up questions with precise requests for additional information. Ideally, incorporating input fields would enable users to provide targeted context.

Response with follow up+input

Please note that introducing input fields would add frontend complexity, so this remains a hypothetical concept for now.

**(2) The Importance of System Visibility**

Lack of visibility into system statistics is a pervasive source of user frustration, spanning both digital and physical experiences. As inherently skeptical users, we doubt and question the system’s integrity when we don’t receive immediate responses.

This concept is aptly described as “Feedback” in Don Norman’s _“The Design of Everyday Things.”_ However, I deliberately avoided using this term, “Feedback” due to its common association with providing constructive criticism.

In its literal sense, “Feedback” refers to a signal responding to input or interaction. Given the intrinsic complexities of AI engines, visualizing the processing dynamics poses significant challenges, particularly when conveying abstract concepts like tokenization.

Despite these limitations, I identified an opportunity for AI to provide users with crucial “feedback” or system visibility. For instance:

Keyword detection in Tellius Demo

When output is generated, highlighting specific elements(keywords) in producing the answer can offer valuable insights. This enhanced visibility enables users to:

*   Gain a deeper understanding of AI’s decision-making process
*   Refine their query-crafting skills
*   **Evolve into “better” users, capable of asking more effective questions (AI improves users)**

### 3. All or Nothing

The final frustration point lies in the rigid, monolithic process of generating output, where users are relegated to passive observers. Once input is processed, users have no opportunity to contribute, forced to merely wait until completion.

I’m Unstoppable

This “all or nothing” approach fuels frustration due to:

*   Prolonged waiting times
*   Frequently unmet expectations

To alleviate this, consider combining solutions explored in this chapter:

*   Providing feedback and visibility into progress
*   Enhancing specificity through improved user input
*   Offering choices to align expectations

Let’s examine sketch ideations of these three elements.

Existing All or Nothing Model

In this traditional approach, users submit queries and must wait idly until the process completes, without opportunity for intervention or refinement.

Existing All or nothing Model

The incremental model introduces:

*   Feedback: displaying progress and system dynamics
*   Visibility: revealing how queries are processed
*   Specificity: enabling users to refine queries through follow-up questions

This model empowers users to adapt and refine their queries, fostering a more collaborative and effective AI interaction experience.

### Conclusion

As the initial euphoria of the AI revolution subsides, reality sets in, revealing a steep disappointment curve. The illusion that AI can effortlessly solve all problems has given way to frustration.

Managing user frustration by setting the right expectations, providing clear visibility, and building Incremental Interaction will make AI products more usable. This approach not only improves AI as a tool but also enhances its users, fostering a symbiotic relationship where AI learns from feedback and users refine their query-crafting skills.
