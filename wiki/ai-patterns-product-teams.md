# 37 AI Patterns for Product Teams: Building Future-Proof AI Products

AI is no longer just a feature or sparkle icon—integrating AI into products fundamentally changes what products are, user expectations, and designer responsibilities. Building future-proof AI products requires battle-tested patterns organized across five product journey phases: onboarding, input, output, refinement, and learning. These patterns provide teams with a shared language to navigate AI's trade-offs effectively.

## Key Patterns & Concepts

- **Onboarding Phase**: Introduce AI capabilities, set expectations, communicate limits; use suggested prompts to show system capabilities
- **Input Phase**: Help users provide context via prompts, images, gestures; structure inputs to reduce friction
- **Output Phase**: Deliver results in appropriate formats (text, image, video); show structured outputs; provide preview options
- **Refinement Phase**: Enable iterative improvement through edit, regenerate, review loops; maintain user control
- **Learning Phase**: System adapts over time through memory, personalization, and feedback mechanisms

## Full Article

AI is no longer "just a feature" or a "sparkle icon". Integrating AI into your product changes what products are, how people expect them to behave, and what responsibilities designers & product teams carry.

Users are now expecting adaptive, multi-modal, intelligent behavior. Concerns surface around trust, transparency, hallucinations, bias, and misuse. New usability issues emerge: vague prompts, ambiguous output, friction in refinement loops.

AI raises new possibilities but also new pitfalls. Product teams need patterns—repeatable, battle-tested solutions—to navigate the trade-offs.

### The 5 Phases Framework

Each AI interaction can be divided into 5 phases:

1. **Onboarding** — How do you introduce AI, what can it do, with what limits?
2. **Input** — How does the user provide context: prompts, images, gestures, etc.?
3. **Output** — How are results delivered: formats (text, image, video), structure, preview?
4. **Refinement** — How can users iteratively improve results: edit, regenerate, review?
5. **Learning** — How does the system adapt over time: memory, personalization, feedback?

Each AI pattern lies in one of these five phases, representing best practices from other AI-native products made available for your implementation.
