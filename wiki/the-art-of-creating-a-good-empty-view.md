## Start to create empty views that turn bad experiences into delightful ones that will set your product apart from the rest.

A good empty view tells a story

For most of us, empty views _(empty states, same same)_ are an afterthought. But they provide so much opportunity for designers to help drive engagement in the product. That’s why it’s important to spend time designing empty views that turn bad experiences into delightful ones.

An empty view is a state in your product that provides no data for the user to consume or interact with. Users can experience an empty view due to many different scenarios such as the following:

*   During on-boarding since it’s their first time using the product. Such as their list of saved items are empty.
*   When the product encounters an error and is unable to complete a task.
*   When information is loading or no information return. Such as an empty search results screen.
*   Due to a user action that removes information. Such as deleting messages in an inbox would result in an empty list of messages.

It’s important to design for these experiences. With that in mind let’s identify what a good empty view should do. I believe it should do three things and three things good.

### Inform

The initial experience teaches users how to use our product and understand where they are in the experience. Also, we want to give the user an impression of how the screen will look once it is filled with data.

Deliver this information in a way that helps them visualise what the screen will look like when it’s populated. Also, try to provide an explanation of the content that lives on this screen and how to get that content.

The 404 page of Medium is a good example of informing the user. https://medium.com/elwexicano

### Entertain

Informing the user will only go so far in getting them to continue to use the product. So, a little entertainment is needed. Do this by showing a little personality and humour to the user. Thinking of the following will help:

*   Can you be creative by doing the unexpected.
*   Can you show the human side of the product and show off your sense of humour.

Turning a frustrating situation into an entertaining one can lead to delighting users. Which provides an experience that makes it stand out from the crowd.

A great example of something different by Calabra Motion. http://bit.ly/2gzlZge

### Trigger

Informing and entertaining users is important but getting them to perform an action that will drive engagement is key to the success of the product. Getting users to perform an action that will help generate content is tricky but the following can help:

*   The language and tone are important so use motivational language such as using “Sell now” instead of “Place item”.
*   Remind the user of the value they will receive from the product. Explain how your product will provide value to them, don’t focus on telling them the features.
*   Provide a simple way to get users to perform the desired action. Having a single call-to-action instead of multiple options is best.

Sailica provide a nice way of triggering an action from the user. https://sailica.com/

### Conclusion

Now that you understand what’s involved in creating good empty views you should go a create them. Turning a bad experience into a potentially good one is something that will set your product apart from others. Isn’t that what you want?!!
